Introducing GPT-6 Astra

A new generation
of intelligence

We’re introducing GPT-6 Astra, the world’s most intelligent and aligned model.

GPT-6 Astra brings together years of research across pre-training, reinforcement learning, and alignment. It reaches new frontiers in computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work.

Astra is rolling out to a limited set of organizations first, then to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as the OpenAI API, Microsoft Azure, and AWS Bedrock.
